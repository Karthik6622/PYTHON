About

I'm a QA engineer who moved upstream — from writing test cases by hand to building the AI systems that write them. At Ascendion Engineering, I work on HP's QAma platform, architecting multi-agent pipelines that read a Jira story, pull in Figma designs and attachments, and produce structured test scenarios and automated test cases with almost no manual authoring.

My toolkit sits at the intersection of QA discipline and agentic engineering: workflow triggering over REST, execution-ID tracking, dual-level status polling, HMAC-SHA256 signed Azure Blob storage, and CrewAI tools that read and write across PDF, DOCX, XLSX and image formats. Before that, I spent three years testing embedded printer firmware at Toshiba — the discipline that still shapes how carefully I verify every pipeline I build.
